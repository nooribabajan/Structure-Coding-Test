First Year Chemistry — Lecture 03

Main Topic: Chemical Yield, Stoichiometric Applications & Process Efficiency. Basic: theoretical, actual and percentage yield. Application: stoichiometry in medicine production and dosage. Mastery: industrial optimisation, yield loss, purity–yield relationships, reaction efficiency, scale-up and quantitative process evaluation.

Learning Outcomes
  1. Theoretical yield، actual yield اور percentage yield میں فرق واضح کرے اور percentage yield calculate کرے۔
  2. Stoichiometry کو pharmaceutical production، dosage اور quantitative formulation میں apply کرے۔
  3. Yield loss، process efficiency اور purity کے باہمی تعلق کو interpret کرے۔
  4. Reaction efficiency اور quantitative process performance کو data کی بنیاد پر evaluate کرے۔
  5. Laboratory scale سے industrial scale تک stoichiometric scale-up calculations کرے۔
  6. Industrial stoichiometric optimisation میں material balance، losses، purity اور yield کو مربوط کرے۔
